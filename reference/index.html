I build clinical AI that has to work when there is a real patient on the other end of it.

Most of what I do is unglamorous. Pipelines, retrieval, evals, the plumbing that decides whether a model is useful or just fluent. I care a lot about the difference.

I started in a wet lab and ended up here, which felt like a detour for about three years and then suddenly did not.

Selected projects

  • RegWatchCompliance

    Four agents that read a codebase for HIPAA violations. Static analysis handles what is provable, a language model handles what is only arguable, and the split between those two is the whole design.

    A World Cup qualification tracker that refuses to publish when its two sources disagree. Built test-first, then checked against an independent simulation to see whether I had actually understood the maths.

    Outbreak data from WHO and OWID, reconciled into one series, with anomaly detection over the top and a map that does not make you work for it.
